## 🚀 **How to Use:**

### **1. Access POS System:**
```
Navigate to: /pos
Or click "POS System" in sidebar
```

### **2. Add Products to Cart:**
- Click on any product card
- Use quantity controls to adjust amounts
- Remove items with trash icon

### **3. Apply Discounts:**
- Click "Apply Discount" toggle
- Select Fixed ($) or Percentage (%)
- Enter discount value

### **4. Select Customer:**
- Choose from dropdown or use Walk-in Customer
- Customer info will be saved with sale

### **5. Choose Payment Method:**
- Click on preferred payment method
- Visual selection with icons

### **6. Complete Sale:**
- Click "Complete Sale" button
- Automatic receipt generation
- Print receipt option
